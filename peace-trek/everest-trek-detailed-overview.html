Everest Trek Detailed Overview

administrator

 

- Detailed Itinerary of Everest Base Camp Trek
- Learn more about Trekking in the Everest Region-


Day 1 - Arrive Kathmandu 1350m / 4355 feet
Arrive in Kathmandu. You’ll be met at the airport by a representative from the Everest Peace Project or our local agency and so look out for a sign with your name on it. You will be taken to the hotel.

You have the rest of the day to either rest and relax or to explore the wonderous chaos that is Kathmandu and the tourist area of Thamel. Thamel is filled with international tourists, backpackers, and pilgrims. There is an abundance of restaurants, hotels, bars, internet cafes, music shops and stores that carry just about anything you may need, including some nice last minute gear for your trek.  

Day 2 - Kathmandu 1350m / 4355 feet
Over breakfast we will have a team meeting and make sure all questions are answered and that everything is prepared for tomorrow's journey. After, we will then explore the sights, sounds, and smells (!) of Kathmandu and the surrounding valley with a very knowledgeable local guide. Upon our return, in the late afternoon, we will have a last minute gear check to make sure everyone has all that they need for the journey. 

Day 3 - fly to Lukla 2850m / 9350 feet, trek to Phakding 2680m / 8790 feet
We take a spectacular 50 minute flight by Twin Otter plane to Lukla. And after a hearty second breakfast, trek to Phakding, a couple of hours walk. The trail traverses the side of the deep Dudh Kosi valley until we meet the base of the valley. - (Approximate time trekking 3 hours) -

Day 4 - to Namche 3450m / 11,315 feet
At Jorsale, a fitting gateway, we enter Sagarmatha National Park. This is a cool damp area since the rugged valley walls block the sun for most of the day. It's not a long day but it is finished with a steep hour and a half or so climb to Namche. Usually we arrive for a late lunch so during the afternoon you have time to discover the village, wash clothes or dive into that book you've been too busy to read. Namche sits in an amphitheater surrounded by mountains, and from here on up we have million dollar views every day. - (Approximate time trekking 4 to 5 hours) -

Day 5 - Namche 3450m / 11,315 feet
This is an acclimatization day so you are free to explore and/or relax. Options are visiting Thame and its gompas, the Everest View Hotel, the National Park Headquarters and Sherpa cultural centre, Khumjung and Khunde with the Hillary hospital, wildlife spotting, or simply eating and relaxing. From here up our itinerary is dictated by the acclimatization process so we have plenty of shorter days walking.

Day 6 - to Tengboche 3860m / 12,687 feet
From Namche it is three-quarters of a day's walk to Tengboche. In the forest en route we often see Himalayan tahr, pheasants, barking deer and musk deer. What a place! From Tengboche there's an extensive panorama including the tip of Everest. It is an added bonus seeing the beautiful monastery and its smiling and spiritual monks. - (Approximate time trekking 5 to 6 hours) -

Day 7 - to Pangboche 3975m / 13,040 feet
In the morning we take a look around the huge new Tengboche gompa. The energetic could also climb part of the ridge behind Tengboche. Pangboche, where few groups stay, is an afternoon's stroll away. It makes more sense, acclimatization-wise to stay at Pangboche than spend a traditional two nights at Tengboche. Pangboche is the highest year-round inhabited village. It also has an old gompa, rich in history, which we have the chance to explore.

Day 8 - to Dingboche 4350m / 14,400 feet
Now we begin passing through high altitude cropping and grazing areas. We have more excellent views of Ama Dablam, and many other mountains. Dingboche has several good lodges and makes a good base for exploring.

Day 9 - Rest / acclimitization day at Dingboche 4350 / 14,400 feet
Alternatively we might make a side trip up to Chukhung, where there are fantastic views down valley towards Shorong Yula and Numbur. We could also trek to Tuglha - the trail to Tuglha is wide with huge vistas of the peaks around. It is an easy 2 hour walk to the lodge at Tuglha. It all depends on how the group is feeling and acclimating. 

Day 10 - Trek to Lobuche 4940m / 16,175 feet
We are really getting up there in elevation. Passing Tuglha we pass a series of beautiful yet somber memorials to some of the lives that the mountains around have claimed over the years. The trail follows the ablation valley so we can peer over the moraine and eyeball the glacier close up. Once at Lobuche we have time to explore or relax.

Day 11 - Trek to Gorak Shep 5164m /  16,942 feet
The final acclimitization stop and the launching pad for our destination: Kala Pattar and Mt. Everest Base Camp.

Day 12 - Climb Kala Pattar 5545m / 18,209 feet, stay Gorak Shep
Everest day! The usual option is the rewarding non-technical climb of Kala Pattar (5545m / 18,209 feet), a day made easier by staying at Gorak Shep after the strenuous hike.

From Kala Pattar the panorama in every direction is staggering, and of course includes Everest. It may be the top of the world, but from Kala Pattar you will feel on top of the world - and Everest feels as if you can reach out and touch it!

Day 13 - Visit Everest Base Camp 5360m / 17,590 feet - stay at Gorak Shep
Base Camp day. We trek to Everest Base Camp where we will meet some Everest Expeditions and the view of the formidable Khumbu Ice Fall is spectacular. We will stay the night at Gorak Shep or perhaps hike back down to Lobuche. 

Day 14 - Trek to Pangboche - 3975m / 13,040 feet
Heading back down feels like you are floating on air.

Day 15 - to Namche 3450m / 11,315 feet
Today we must reach Namche. Whatever the route we take it will be a classic day's walk. There is a hot shower and a beer waiting for you.

Day 16 - to Lukla 2850m / 9350 feet
This is also longer trekking day. If conditions are good this will take around 5-6 hours actual walking. The Lukla lodges gently prepare us for tomorrow - back to civilization. We also have the option of staying at Choplung, then tomorrow morning the hike to Lukla takes less than 40 minutes.

Day 17 - fly to Kathmandu 1350m / 4355 feet
We fly by plane to Kathmandu in the morning. Occasionally flights are delayed by bad weather. Lukla has a great laundry service and lots of hot water.

Day 18 - Kathmandu 1350m / 4355 feet
A day to relax and enjoy being back in relative civilization! Make sure to buy those last minute gifts for yourself, friends, and family members. If you are still in an exploring mood we are happy to suggest other sight-seeing destinations and can provide you with an experienced guide. Beautiful, exotic, and fun options are: visiting the ancient city of Bhaktapur, going to Patan's Durbar Square, experiencing the Buddhist center of Boudhanath. The Kathmandu valley has seven UNESCO World Heritage Sites. You will not run out of things to do!

Day 19 - Farewell
We take you to the airport for your flight home. If you wish to stay longer we can offer plenty of suggestions of what to do and where to go. 


Note: You should bring a smile, your sense of adventure and a curiosity to learn about other cultures and ways of life. 

- The Itinerary is subject to slight variation depending on conditions in the mountains. Remaining flexible is always important.
