Special Screening of the History-Making Everest Film

Host a special screening of the award winning film - Everest: A Climb for Peace with Lance Trumbull. Narrated by Orlando Bloom and hailed as a "tremendous achievement" by the Dalai Lama. Everest: A Climb for Peace is not just a typical Everest film, but a socially relevant documentary about peace, war, and the human spirit, an inspirational film, which also has some of the most incredible Everest footage ever shot.

- Lance Trumbull directed, produced, and co-wrote this award winning film. Lance was also expedition leader of the climb. Hear first hand what it is like to organize a historic expedition, to climb Mt. Everest, and the filmmaking process of producing and directing a documentary film.
