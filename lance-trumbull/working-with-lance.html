Working With Lance Trumbull

administrator

Lance Trumbull Will:

  • - Be readily available to your organizations's planners & organizers
  • - Familiarize himself with your organization and/or team
  • - Craft his presentation to your specific needs
  • - Conduct a walk-through at least 60 minutes prior to the event
  • - Meet with set-up staff and other presenters to ensure proper coordination
  • - Engage the audience, employing humor & personal anecdotes
  • - Deliver information specific to the group's needs
  • - Be accessible to the audience to address their individual concerns and questions
  • - Be available for follow-up questions via phone, Skype, or email
