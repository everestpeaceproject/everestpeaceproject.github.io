Lance Trumbull - Diversity & Motivational Speaker

Looking for an Inspirational & dynamic diversity speaker?

Book a lecture with Lance Trumbull. Lance is a professional lecturer and motivational - diversity speaker. He is the founder and executive director of The Everest Peace Project - a peace organization in Portland, Oregon, was the expedition leader for the historic Everest Peace Climb, and is the director and producer of the award winning film - Everest: A Climb for Peace, which was narrated by Orlando Bloom and endorsed by the Dalai Lama. Lance's history-making Everest expedition brought Palestinian and Israelis together on Mt. Everest and was also endorsed by the UN.
