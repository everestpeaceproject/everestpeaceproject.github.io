Motivational Speaking and Film Screening

administrator

Book an inspirational and motivational talk with Lance Trumbull - you can also book a screening of the film Everest: A Climb for Peace.

Lance Trumbull is a motivational speaker, filmmaker, adventurer, and author. He is the executive director of The Everest Peace Project, a peace organization in Beaverton, Oregon.

Lance was the organizer, creator, and the expedition leader for the Everest Peace Project's history-making Everest Peace Climb. He helped successfully co-lead 10 people to the summit of Everest. The Everest Peace Climb made world-history by assembling one of the most diverse Everest teams ever created, which consisted of people from diverse faiths, countries, and cultures - including Palestinian and Israeli men. Lance helped put together every aspect of the project, climb, and film, including assembling the multi-cultural team, finding sponsors, and producing and directing the award-winnng documentary.


Lance Trumbull in Ladakh India. The area where he became inspired to create The Everest Peace Project

He went to school at UC Berkeley, where he studied religion under world famous scholar Huston Smith. Being a Buddhist he has actively tried to lead a compassionate and mindful life. His passion has been mountain climbing and exploring - he has spent many months trekking and climbing through and on mountains in Nepal, Tibet, China, Mongolia, Russia, and India. In 2002 he climbed Mt. Elbrus in Russia, summited a couple of 6000 meter peaks in Ladakh, and spent time on Everest in Tibet. In July 2005 and 2011, he successfully led the Kilimanjaro Climb for Peace teams to the roof of Africa - and on May 18th, 2006 his Everest Climb for Peace summited 10 climbers.

Lance just wrote his first children's book. The inspiration for it came from his young son, Kaden.

Special Screening of the History-Making Everest Film
Host a special screening of the award winning film - Everest: A Climb for Peace with Lance Trumbull. Narrated by Orlando Bloom and hailed as a "tremendous achievement" by the Dalai Lama. Everest: A Climb for Peace is not just a typical Everest film, but a socially relevant documentary about peace, war, and the human spirit, an inspirational film, which also has some of the most incredible Everest footage ever shot.

- Lance Trumbull directed, produced, and co-wrote this award winning film. Lance was also expedition leader of the climb. Hear first hand what it is like to organize a historic expedition, to climb Mt. Everest, and the filmmaking process of producing and directing a documentary film.
