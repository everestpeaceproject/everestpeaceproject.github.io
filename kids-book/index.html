Everest: A Climb for Peace — A Children's Book by Lance Trumbull

Everest: A Climb for Peace — A Children's Book

Everest: A Climb for Peace - A Children's Book chronicles the true, inspirational journey of "Peace Climbers" from different faiths and cultures as they climb together to the summit of the tallest mountain in the world.

Featuring Palestinian climber Ali Bushnaq and Israeli climbers Dudu Yifrah and Micha Yaniv alongside expedition leader Lance Trumbull, the story shows how teamwork, friendship, and cooperation overcome decades of conflict on the world's highest peak.
